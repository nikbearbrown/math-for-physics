**Fig 07-01 — Projectile path: independent components, nonzero apex velocity.**
- Concept: componentwise differentiation makes the independence of horizontal and vertical motion a theorem — at the apex v_x ≠ 0.
- Type: parametric trajectory with velocity vectors at sampled instants.
- Shows: the parabolic path x=(v₀cosθ)t, y=(v₀sinθ)t−½gt², velocity arrows at launch, apex, and descent, each resolved into constant v_x and shrinking/growing v_y; apex arrow drawn purely horizontal.
- SCOPE include: trajectory, 3 velocity vectors with components, the apex "v_x still nonzero" callout. EXCLUDE: centripetal case, related rates.

**Fig 07-02 — Centripetal acceleration from a rotating vector.**
- Concept: differentiating r(t)=r cos(ωt)î + r sin(ωt)ĵ twice gives a = −ω²r, pointing to the center; a_c = v²/r.
- Type: circle with position, velocity (tangent), and acceleration (inward) vectors.
- Shows: the circle, position vector r to a point, velocity v tangent (perpendicular to r), acceleration a pointing back to center, with |v|=rω constant and a_c=v²/r boxed.
- SCOPE include: circle, r/v/a vectors, perpendicularity of v to r, the boxed a_c=v²/r. EXCLUDE: jet numbers, angular-vs-centripetal aside.
