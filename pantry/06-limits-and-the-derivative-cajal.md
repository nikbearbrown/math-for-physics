# CAJAL — Ch.06 Limits and the Derivative

**Fig 06-01 — Secant tilts into tangent as Δt → 0.**
- Concept: the instantaneous rate is the limit of the difference quotient — the secant line tilting toward the tangent as the second point slides in.
- Type: curve with a family of secant lines converging to a tangent.
- Shows: the parabola x(t)=½gt², a fixed point at t, several secants to points at t+Δt for shrinking Δt, the limiting tangent line, and the difference-quotient label Δx/Δt → dx/dt.
- SCOPE include: curve, fixed point, 2–3 secants, the tangent, the limit annotation. EXCLUDE: rule list, second derivative.

**Fig 06-02 — Position, velocity, acceleration as successive slopes.**
- Concept: differentiating x(t) gives v(t) as its slope; differentiating again gives constant a(t).
- Type: stacked triple plot sharing a time axis.
- Shows: x(t)=½gt² (parabola), v(t)=gt (rising line, the slope of x), a(t)=g (flat line, the slope of v), aligned vertically with arrows linking "slope of" between panels.
- SCOPE include: the three stacked curves, the slope-of linkage arrows, free-fall labels. EXCLUDE: chain-rule rotation example, numeric 294 m/s callout clutter.
