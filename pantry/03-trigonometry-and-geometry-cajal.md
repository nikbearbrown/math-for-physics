# CAJAL — Ch.03 Trigonometry and Geometry

**Fig 03-01 — The unit circle as home of sine, cosine, tangent.**
- Concept: for any angle θ, (cosθ, sinθ) are the coordinates of a point on the unit circle; tangent is the slope.
- Type: unit-circle construction diagram.
- Shows: circle radius 1, angle θ from +x axis, the point (cosθ, sinθ), dropped perpendiculars giving cos as horizontal leg and sin as vertical leg, arc length = θ (radians).
- SCOPE include: circle, radius, θ, cos/sin legs, the point, arc=θ label. EXCLUDE: full tangent-line segment clutter, multiple angles, periodicity wave.

**Fig 03-02 — Resolving weight on an incline (which gets the sine?).**
- Concept: build the right triangle and read the ratios — the down-slope component carries sinθ, the into-surface component carries cosθ.
- Type: incline + force-decomposition diagram.
- Shows: ramp at θ, weight mg straight down as hypotenuse, components mg sinθ (down slope) and mg cosθ (into surface), the angle θ located at the surface normal.
- SCOPE include: ramp, mg vector, the two resolved components, θ placement. EXCLUDE: friction, numeric force, projectile.

**Fig 03-03 — Small-angle approximation sinθ ≈ θ.**
- Concept: for small angles in radians the arc (θ), the chord, and sinθ converge; sinθ ≈ θ.
- Type: dual panel — circle close-up + sinθ-vs-θ curve.
- Shows: zoomed arc where arc length θ and vertical drop sinθ nearly coincide, beside a plot of y=θ (line) and y=sinθ (curve) hugging near the origin and diverging by ~θ=0.5.
- SCOPE include: the converging arc/sine geometry, the line-vs-curve plot, the divergence region. EXCLUDE: cosθ≈1−θ²/2 curve, Taylor series.
