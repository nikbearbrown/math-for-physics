# CAJAL — Ch.05 Functions, Graphs, and Power Laws

**Fig 05-01 — A catalog of four function shapes.**
- Concept: linear, quadratic, power-law, and exponential each have a characteristic shape and a physical fingerprint.
- Type: small-multiples curve panel (4 mini-plots on shared axes style).
- Shows: y=mx+b (line, constant rate), y=ax² (parabola, constant acceleration), y=ax^b (power-law scaling), y=a·bˣ (exponential, constant fractional change), each captioned with its fingerprint.
- SCOPE include: the four curves, their forms, one-line fingerprints. EXCLUDE: log-log straightening (its own figure), metabolic data.

**Fig 05-02 — A power law straightens on log–log axes (free fall, slope = 2).**
- Concept: plotting log y vs log x turns y = ax^b into a straight line whose slope is the exponent b.
- Type: log–log line plot with slope read-off.
- Shows: free-fall data d = 4.9t² at t = 1, 10, 100 s plotted as (log t, log d) on a straight line, the rise (4 decades) over run (2 decades), slope b = 2 annotated, intercept log a = log 4.9.
- SCOPE include: the three plotted points, the line, the rise/run slope triangle, slope=2. EXCLUDE: the four-shape catalog, semi-log exponential, contested metabolic exponent.
