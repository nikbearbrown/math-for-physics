# CAJAL — Ch.02 Algebra and Equations in Physics

**Fig 02-01 — The Atwood machine: reading the symbol at its limits.**
- Concept: the symbolic solution a = (m₂−m₁)g/(m₁+m₂) can be *read* at limiting cases a number never could.
- Type: labeled mechanism diagram (pulley + two blocks) with a side panel of limit readings.
- Shows: the two blocks, tension T and weights, the free-body equations, the boxed result, and three reads: equal masses → a=0; m₁→0 → a→g; a always < g.
- SCOPE include: pulley/blocks/rope, the two force equations, boxed a, the three limit reads. EXCLUDE: numeric a for 3 & 5 kg, incline problem.

**Fig 02-02 — Galileo's square–cube law.**
- Concept: scale a shape by L and area∝L², volume∝L³, so stress = weight/cross-section ∝ L — bigger animals load their bones harder.
- Type: scaling comparison (small cube vs cube of double side) with a proportionality ladder.
- Shows: side L vs 2L; area ×4, volume ×8, stress ×2; the three proportionalities as a stacked ladder.
- SCOPE include: two cubes, the ×4/×8/×2 factors, stress∝L conclusion. EXCLUDE: spring period, elephant illustration detail.

**Fig 02-03 — The sliding criterion tanθ > μ.**
- Concept: dividing out m gives a = g(sinθ − μcosθ); the block slips precisely when tanθ exceeds μ.
- Type: incline diagram + small inset of the slip threshold.
- Shows: block on ramp angle θ, the driving (mg sinθ) and friction (μmg cosθ) terms, and the criterion tanθ > μ with the critical angle θ_c = arctan μ.
- SCOPE include: ramp, the two competing terms, the tanθ>μ threshold. EXCLUDE: numeric values, full free-body of normal force vectors.
