# CAJAL — Ch.04 Vectors and Vector Algebra

**Fig 04-01 — Tip-to-tail addition with components (the pilot and the wind).**
- Concept: velocities add as arrows, component by component — not as magnitudes.
- Type: vector-addition diagram on E–N axes.
- Shows: airspeed vector (~84.9 î + 84.9 ĵ, NE), wind vector (~17.7 î − 17.7 ĵ, toward SE), placed tip-to-tail, resultant ground velocity (102.6 î + 67.2 ĵ) ≈ 123 kn at ~33° N of E.
- SCOPE include: the two input vectors, tip-to-tail construction, the resultant with its heading. EXCLUDE: dot/cross product, full knot grid.

**Fig 04-02 — The dot product as projection.**
- Concept: A·B = |A||B|cosφ is the length of B times how much of A lies along it — a shadow.
- Type: projection diagram.
- Shows: vectors A and B from a common origin, angle φ, the perpendicular dropped from A's tip onto B's line, the projection |A|cosφ marked along B, and the alignment law boxed.
- SCOPE include: A, B, φ, the projection segment, the formula. EXCLUDE: component formula arithmetic, cross product.

**Fig 04-03 — The cross product as oriented area + right-hand rule.**
- Concept: |A×B| = |A||B|sinφ is the area of the parallelogram A and B span; direction is the right-hand-rule normal.
- Type: parallelogram + normal-arrow diagram.
- Shows: A and B spanning a shaded parallelogram, the height |B|sinφ, the area label, and A×B as an arrow out of the plane (right-hand rule), with anticommutativity note.
- SCOPE include: parallelogram, height, area = |A||B|sinφ, the normal arrow. EXCLUDE: determinant expansion, torque numbers.
