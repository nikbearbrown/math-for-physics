# CAJAL figure report — Chapter 10: Linear Systems and Matrices

Two figures planned. Both serve the chapter's central geometric claim: a 2×2 linear
system is two lines, the solution is their crossing, and the determinant is the number
that decides whether they cross.

## Figure 10.1 — A 2×2 system as two intersecting lines
- **Concept:** A system of two linear equations is two straight lines in the (x,y) plane;
  the simultaneous solution is the single point where they cross.
- **Type:** Line plot / coordinate diagram (two lines on x–y axes).
- **What it shows:** The worked Exercise-1 system 3x + 2y = 12 and x − y = 1, drawn as two
  lines crossing at the solution (2, 1). The crossing point is marked with the red accent
  dot and labelled "(2, 1) — solution". Axis labels x and y; each line labelled with its
  equation. A short note ties the crossing to "both equations true at once."
- **SCOPE — include:** two lines; the labelled crossing point; equation labels; x and y
  axes with a couple of gridline ticks; one-line caption that the crossing is the solution.
- **SCOPE — exclude:** the determinant (that is Fig 10.2); the pan/physics setup; Cramer's
  rule algebra; any 3×3 content; shading of regions.

## Figure 10.2 — The determinant decides: lines that cross vs. lines that don't
- **Concept:** det A ≠ 0 means the two lines cross at one point (unique solution); det A = 0
  means the lines are parallel (no solution, or the same line). The determinant is also the
  signed area of the parallelogram spanned by the coefficient rows — zero area = no crossing.
- **Type:** Two-panel comparison diagram.
- **What it shows:** LEFT panel — two lines crossing once, with the coefficient rows drawn as
  two arrows from the origin spanning a parallelogram of nonzero area (det ≠ 0, "ONE
  SOLUTION"). RIGHT panel — two parallel lines that never meet, with the two row-arrows
  collinear so the parallelogram collapses to a line of zero area (det = 0, "NO SOLUTION").
- **SCOPE — include:** the two contrasting panels; the parallelogram-area reading on the left
  and its collapse on the right; the det ≠ 0 / det = 0 labels; minimal axes.
- **SCOPE — exclude:** numeric determinant arithmetic; 3×3 volume; Cramer numerators; the
  infinitely-many-solutions sub-case (mention only in caption, do not draw a third panel).
