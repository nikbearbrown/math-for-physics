# CAJAL — Ch.01 Units, Dimensions, and Estimation

**Fig 01-01 — Dimensional homogeneity as an error check.**
- Concept: a dimensionally inconsistent equation is certainly wrong; a consistent one is merely allowed.
- Type: side-by-side dimension-tracking table / comparison panel.
- Shows: the consistent candidate s = ½at² (both sides L) beside the wrong candidate s = ½at (LHS = L, RHS = LT⁻¹), with the base-dimension exponents stacked.
- SCOPE include: the two candidate equations, the [s]=L target, the worked dimension of each RHS, a verdict row. EXCLUDE: numbers/units, the Π-theorem, conversions.

**Fig 01-02 — Deriving the pendulum period by matching dimension exponents.**
- Concept: matching the exponent of each base dimension solves for the powers a, b, c with no equation of motion.
- Type: exponent-matching schematic (matrix-style rows L, M, T).
- Shows: Tp = C·ℓᵃgᵇmᶜ, the exponent equations (c=0, −2b=1, a+b=0), and the result Tp = C√(ℓ/g).
- SCOPE include: variable list with dimensions, the three matched constraints, the conclusion. EXCLUDE: numerical period, C=2π proof, Buckingham general statement.

**Fig 01-03 — A power amplifies relative uncertainty.**
- Concept: the relative uncertainty in a product of powers is the sum of input relative uncertainties weighted by exponents; a cube triples it.
- Type: horizontal relative-error bar chart.
- Shows: δd/d = 1.9% for the measured diameter vs δV/V = 5.8% (= 3×1.9%) for V = (π/6)d³, the factor-of-3 amplification labeled.
- SCOPE include: the two bars, the ×3 factor, the worked sphere example values. EXCLUDE: quadrature combination, Fermi estimate.
