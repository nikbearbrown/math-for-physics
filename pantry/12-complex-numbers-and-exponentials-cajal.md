# CAJAL figure report — Chapter 12: Complex Numbers and Exponentials

Two figures. One grounds a complex number as a point/arrow in the plane with both
rectangular and polar coordinates; the other is the chapter's engine — Euler's formula on
the unit circle and the fact that cosine is the horizontal shadow of a rotating phasor.

## Figure 12.1 — A complex number in rectangular and polar form
- **Concept:** z = a + bi is a point (a, b) in the complex plane; the same point is an arrow
  of length r = |z| at angle θ, so a = r cos θ and b = r sin θ. Two coordinate systems, one
  number.
- **Type:** Coordinate diagram in the complex plane (single point/arrow with annotations).
- **What it shows:** Axes labelled Re (horizontal) and Im (vertical). The worked point
  z = 3 + 2i drawn as a red arrow from the origin to (3, 2). Dashed legs show the rectangular
  parts a = 3 and b = 2; an arc at the origin marks the argument θ = atan2(2,3) ≈ 33.7°; the
  arrow itself is labelled with the modulus r = √13. Both forms printed: z = 3 + 2i and
  z = r(cos θ + i sin θ).
- **SCOPE — include:** Re/Im axes; the arrow to (3,2); dashed rectangular legs a, b; the angle
  arc θ; the modulus label r; both-form annotation.
- **SCOPE — exclude:** multiplication-as-rotation (sketch only in caption); Euler's formula
  (that is Fig 12.2); conjugate; the unit circle.

## Figure 12.2 — Euler's formula and the phasor that casts a cosine
- **Concept:** e^(iθ) = cos θ + i sin θ traces the unit circle; a point e^(iωt) circles at
  rate ω, and its horizontal shadow (the real part) is cos ωt. Rotation projects to oscillation.
- **Type:** Combined diagram — unit circle (left) linked to a cosine plot (right).
- **What it shows:** LEFT — the unit circle with a red phasor arrow e^(iθ) to a point on the
  circle at angle θ; its coordinates labelled (cos θ, sin θ); a dashed horizontal drop to the
  Re axis marks cos θ. RIGHT — the cosine curve cos θ plotted vs. θ, with a dashed tie-line
  from the phasor's height carried across to the matching point on the curve, showing the
  shadow tracing out the cosine. Re/Im axes on the circle; θ and cos θ axes on the plot.
- **SCOPE — include:** unit circle with phasor; (cos θ, sin θ) labels; the projection drop to
  Re axis; the linked cosine curve; one tie-line connecting circle-shadow to curve-point.
- **SCOPE — exclude:** the power-series derivation; decay/inward spiral (mention in caption);
  phasor addition; sine curve as a second plot (keep to cosine = real part).
