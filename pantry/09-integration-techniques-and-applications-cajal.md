# CAJAL — Ch.09 Integration Techniques and Applications

**Fig 09-01 — Slicing a disk into rings for ∫ r² dm.**
- Concept: the setup step — choose a slice matching the symmetry (a ring), write dm = σ·2πr dr, integrate r²dm to get ½MR².
- Type: disk-with-annular-slice mass-element diagram.
- Shows: a disk of radius R, one highlighted thin ring at radius r of thickness dr, circumference 2πr, the contribution dm = (M/πR²)·2πr dr, and the result I = ½MR².
- SCOPE include: disk, the ring slice, dm expression, the boxed ½MR². EXCLUDE: rod case, substitution/parts technique, dam.

**Fig 09-02 — Hydrostatic force on a dam (depth strips).**
- Concept: pressure varies with depth, so total force is an accumulation — slice into horizontal strips, dF = ρgy·w dy, integrate to ½ρgwh².
- Type: dam cross-section with a horizontal pressure strip and a pressure-vs-depth triangle.
- Shows: the wetted face of width w to depth h, one strip at depth y of height dy, the linearly growing pressure p=ρgy (triangular profile), and the result F = ½ρgwh² (∝ h²).
- SCOPE include: dam face, the strip, the triangular pressure profile, the boxed ½ρgwh². EXCLUDE: ring/disk case, center-of-mass example.
